## The rule, corrected

The rule previously written in O-24 ("the settle predicate must be strictly
weaker than the assertion — wait for focus to stop moving") is WRONG and was
the trap the 03-17 executor nearly fell into. Quiescence settles on the failure
state: an absent focus and a pending dead key are both stable indefinitely.

The rule now written into `waitFor`'s own documentation in the lane:

> Poll for the condition you are about to assert, to a bounded deadline, and
> report the last observation if it never holds.

That does not make a row vacuous. A condition that never holds still fails —
now with what it actually saw, which the old fixed-sleep read could not report.

There are exactly two waiting mechanisms in the file and there must not be a
third: `settledFocus(handle, { until })` for AX focus, `waitFor` for everything
else. A6's ad-hoc polling loop was folded into the former.

## A3 was a lane race, not a product defect

The conflict heading MOUNTS before the app moves focus onto it. Reading focus
the instant the heading appeared sampled the gap in between. Polling for the
focus the check asserts on makes A3 10/10; the product moves focus correctly.

constructed in the real `bootstrap()` and passed to `QuickEntryWindowController`.
