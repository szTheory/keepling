<design_guidance>
Measured facts, established before this plan was written. Verify them; do not
trust the line numbers, which drift.

* The packaged build IS reproducible. Two consecutive `pnpm package:desktop`
  runs on an unchanged tree both produced
  `applicationDigestSha256=937b279ce76952f0...` and
  `executableDigestSha256=cb1ff05197251895...`. So a digest change means the
  inputs really changed, and a row that fails on a stable digest is racing --
  it is not looking at different bytes.

* **A3 standalone** failed with: `could not reach the Discard Draft button by
  keyboard within 24 tab presses (focus stopped on AXTextField "What do you
  want to keep?")`. That label is `CAPTURE_FIELD_LABEL`, the Quick Entry
  capture field itself -- so traversal cycled the window and the Discard Draft
  button was never in it. The button only exists once the draft is non-empty.
  The row does:

      postKeys(handle, 'text:Draft to discard')
      await sleep(300)
      await tabUntil(handle, 'the Discard Draft button', ...)

  If the keystrokes have not been processed and re-rendered within 300ms, the
  button does not exist yet and `tabUntil` exhausts its budget against a
  window that is working correctly. **The precondition to wait for is the
  field's own value, or the button's existence -- not 300ms.**

* **A3 inside `--all`** failed differently: `the conflict moves focus to its
  own heading ... focus was on nothing`, at the 10s `settledFocus` deadline,
  in a row that took 29.5s against a standalone-tuned 14.6-16.5s. That is the
  same root in its deadline form, and it is exactly what O-32 predicted: fixed
  deadlines tuned standalone, consumed in `--all`, which runs ~1.6x slower.

* 31 non-polling `sleep()` calls remain in the file (36 total, minus the five
  inside polling helpers around lines 564, 573, 636 and the process-exit loop
  at 454). 03-18 converted the reads. The ACTIONS were not converted.

The rule, stated exactly:

> Before acting on the product, wait for the observable consequence of the
> PREVIOUS action. Before asserting, wait for the condition being asserted.
> A fixed `sleep()` is only defensible where there is genuinely nothing
> observable to wait for -- and then it must say so, in a comment, naming the
> unobservable.

Concrete shapes to convert, found by grep rather than by line number:

  - `postKeys(... 'text:X')` then `sleep(N)` then anything -- wait for the
    field to actually contain X.
  - `postKeys(... accelerator)` then `sleep(N)` then `tabUntil`/`findNode` --
    wait for the window or element the accelerator was supposed to produce.
  - `sleep(N)` then `postKeys('space'/'return')` on a dialog -- wait for the
    dialog's own focused control first.

On deadlines (O-32): do not hand-tune each one again. Either scale every
bounded wait by a single lane-wide factor when running `--all`, or raise them
uniformly to a value that is generous under load -- a correct wait costs
nothing when the condition is already true, because it returns immediately.
The cost of a long deadline is paid only on genuine failure; the cost of a
short one is paid at random, forever.
</design_guidance>
