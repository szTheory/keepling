# Brand and Naming Brief

## Working brand idea

> A small, distinctive, playful object or companion that quietly keeps commitments safe.

The mark should combine **trusted steward** with **playful familiar**. It should be calm, dependable, memorable, warm, and slightly characterful—never childish, paternalistic, corporate, surveillance-coded, or generically "AI."

AI belongs in descriptors and capability messaging, not necessarily in the timeless product mark.

## Product identity

The product is:

- human-first personal GTD;
- quietly agent-native;
- open source and self-hostable;
- meticulous about reliability, recovery, and user control;
- technically rigorous without presenting as developer infrastructure;
- native-feeling on each platform;
- crafted and intentional rather than feature-maximal.

The brand should support future labels such as `{Name} Cloud`, `{Name} Sync`, `{Name} CLI`, `{Name} MCP`, `{Name} for iPhone`, and `{Name} for Mac` without sounding corporate or strained.

## Avoided verbal territory

Avoid the obvious crowded category and benefit vocabulary in the mark:

- todo, task, list, productivity, GTD;
- focus, flow, plan, day, done;
- AI, agent, copilot, assistant;
- tick/check puns;
- names that sound like Things, Todoist, TickTick, OmniFocus, Superlist, Motion, Routine, Linear, Sunsama, Akiflow, Amie, or Vikunja.

## Avoided visual territory

- Things-like blue box.
- Todoist-like red completion mark.
- Calendar grids or literal checkmarks.
- Linear-like dark/desaturated developer-tool styling.
- Purple/blue AI glow, sparkles, magic-wand imagery, neural brains, robots.
- A visually loud identity that prevents the interface from feeling native and calm.

Potential opening: warm ink/cream with an unusual restrained accent, a strong monochrome silhouette, subtle tactile character, and a symbol readable at 16 px. Final colors follow naming and identity exploration rather than preceding them.

## Naming territories

### Primary: trusted steward × playful familiar

Emotional vocabulary seeds—not candidate names:

- keeper, ward, witness, guide, harbor, lantern;
- pocket object, household tool, small creature, charm, token;
- holding, remembering, returning, accompanying, guarding;
- soft rhythmic phonemes and a friendly spoken cadence.

Failure modes:

- security/finance product;
- parenting or therapy tone;
- infantilizing mascot;
- cute but untrustworthy;
- a character that does not travel internationally;
- an implied autonomous assistant that undermines user agency.

### Secondary references

- **Craft and finish:** tactile, fitted, carefully made; risk of maker cliché.
- **Temporal landscape:** spacious forward motion; risk of calendar/travel abstraction.
- **Living ecology:** quiet growth; risk of wellness/knowledge-garden cliché.
- **Clear signal:** inspectable and precise; risk of observability/dev-tool positioning.

## Objective scorecard

| Criterion | Weight | Pass condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Legal distinctiveness | 18 | No high-risk similar mark for related software/services after broad search |
| Namespace viability | 15 | Credible canonical domain/GitHub identity and workable App Store/package strategy |
| Emotional fit | 13 | Conveys trusted, playful calm without explanation |
| Pronunciation | 10 | At least 4/5 English speakers pronounce it consistently from text |
| Spelling | 10 | At least 4/5 spell it after hearing it once |
| Searchability | 10 | Exact/phonetic searches are not dominated by software or notorious meanings |
| International screen | 9 | No serious negative, vulgar, political, or misleading meaning in launch languages |
| Product-family fit | 8 | Works with Cloud, Sync, CLI, MCP, and native-client labels |
| Visual-symbol potential | 7 | Distinctive monochrome 16 px silhouette without check/calendar/sparkle |

Hard-kill any candidate with a serious related-software trademark collision, harmful cross-language meaning, unstable pronunciation, or no credible canonical identity. A missing `.com` is not automatically fatal; an apologetic or confusing domain construction is.

- Current task/productivity naming is crowded across literal behavior words, category compounds, benefit verbs, spatial abstractions, and invented/proper-name forms. ## Candidate-generation and clearance funnel

1. Lock desired traits and anti-traits.
2. Generate equal-sized sets across at least three territories to prevent anchoring.
3. Blind-test pronunciation, heard-once spelling, phone-call usability, plural/verb awkwardness, and unaided emotional response.
4. Sweep exact, phonetic, stem, translation, and compound collisions across web, GitHub, App Stores, npm, Hex, and domains.
5. Run USPTO/WIPO and relevant state/national/common-law knockout searches.
6. Screen likely launch languages with native speakers before explaining the intended meaning.
7. Pressure-test icon silhouette, favicon, terminal command, package scope, README, podcast mention, notification copy, and product-family extensions.
8. Record each query, date, spelling variant, jurisdiction, result, and disposition in a clearance ledger.
9. Have counsel review the final 2–3 before public reveal, then acquire accounts/domains together.

"No result" always means "not found by this search," never "legally clear."

## Design-system relationship

- Brand supplies a restrained semantic accent, iconography, voice, and identity primitives.
- Platform UI uses system typography and native interaction conventions where possible.
- React and SwiftUI share semantic token intent, not identical layout or lowest-common-denominator controls.
- Design tokens should cover color roles, typography roles, spacing rhythm, radii, elevation, motion duration/easing, focus, and semantic status without prematurely creating hundreds of aliases.
- Microanimation communicates capture, completion, undo, conflict, sync, and state change; Reduce Motion receives a meaningful non-motion substitute.
- Native feel and usability outrank decorative brand consistency.

## Next naming question

Choose the word-form bias before generating candidates:

- recognizable real word that gains meaning through the product;
- rare/obscure real word that rewards discovery;
- pronounceable coined word with maximal distinctiveness;
- balanced mix for the first candidate round.
